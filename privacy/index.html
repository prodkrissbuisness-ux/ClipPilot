Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

ClipPilot is a tool, run by CommonPilot, that turns popular Twitch clips into captioned YouTube Shorts and uploads them to a YouTube channel. This page explains what information ClipPilot uses, why, and what control you have over it.

Information from YouTube and Google

ClipPilot only connects to a YouTube channel when the channel owner signs in with Google and approves it. Depending on what is approved, ClipPilot can:

  • Upload videos to the channel (YouTube upload permission).
  • Read basic channel information, such as the channel name and the titles and view counts of its videos, to check uploads and learn which titles work (YouTube read-only permission).
  • Edit the titles of videos that ClipPilot uploaded (YouTube manage permission).

ClipPilot does not read your email, contacts, comments, messages, payment details, or anything else in your Google account. It does not delete videos.

How that information is stored

The sign-in token that Google gives ClipPilot is stored only on the computer that runs ClipPilot. It is never sold, shared with other people or companies, or used for advertising. ClipPilot keeps a small log of the videos it uploaded (video ID, title and upload time) so it doesn't upload the same clip twice.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClipPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

ClipPilot uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel to ClipPilot, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove ClipPilot's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also email us and we will delete any stored token and upload log for your channel.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this site, we store your email address and where you came from (for example, a link in a Short). We only use it to tell you when ClipPilot is ready. We don't sell it or share it. Email us to have it removed.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics uses cookies. You can block them in your browser settings.

Twitch clips

ClipPilot uses public clips and public clip information (such as titles and view counts) from Twitch. It does not access any private Twitch account data.

Children

ClipPilot is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
